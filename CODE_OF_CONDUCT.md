# Code of Conduct

Helion follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Helion a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Report abuse to the owner: **@saksham-45** (GitHub). Until more maintainers are listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`, they are the enforcement contact.

This is a technical project. Disagreement about CAD, RTL, or architecture is expected. Personal attacks, doxxing, and vendor-IP dumps are not.
