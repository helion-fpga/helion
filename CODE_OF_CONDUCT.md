# Code of Conduct

Helion follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, Discussions,
reviews, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Helion a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## In practice

This is a technical project, and disagreement about CAD, RTL or architecture
is expected and welcome. Argue about the work, not the person. Personal
attacks, harassment, doxxing, and posting vendor IP or other material you do
not have the right to share are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the owner,
[@saksham-45](https://github.com/saksham-45) on GitHub. Until more
maintainers are named in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), the owner is the
enforcement contact and follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
Reports are handled confidentially.
