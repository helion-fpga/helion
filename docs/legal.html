Legal fence

Helion is an original FPGA family and CAD. Vendor user guides are a checklist of capabilities, not a source tree to copy.

Do not add Project X-Ray, vendor Tcl, UNISIM names, or 7-series / UltraScale / Lattice / Intel bitstream backends.

Do not ship AXI interconnect as Helion IP — the buses are Helion-MM and Helion-ST.

Do not use vendor trademarks as product names in the UI or docs.

What is original

  • HAD parts (HL10T-C32-1 and kin) and FeatureMap frames
  • HNF netlist, Session Tcl, .hbits, .hckp
  • helion-sim / helion-fabric / IEEE 1149.1 TAP in helion-hw
  • The desktop IDE as an eframe shell over IdeModel
