License

Helion is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later). If you distribute a modified version, or let people use a modified version over a network, you must offer them its source under the same license. Releases up to 3.0.0 were published under MIT OR Apache-2.0, and those releases keep that license.

Third-party code keeps its own license: the vendored Rune UI framework in third_party/rune is MIT, and example designs carry their upstream licenses (for example, Ibex is Apache-2.0).

Trademarks

"Helion" and the Helion logo are trademarks of Saksham Srivastava. The license covers the code, not the brand: a fork or modified redistribution must use a different name, and must not imply affiliation. A neutral attribution such as "based on Helion" is welcome. The full policy is TRADEMARKS.md.

The legal fence

Helion is an original FPGA architecture and CAD suite, not a wrapper around a vendor tool. Public vendor user guides are a checklist of capabilities, never a source to copy. Device facts come from HAD data files, not from vendor databases.

Not allowed: proprietary vendor IP, libraries or databases; vendor Tcl scripts or UNISIM libraries copied in; vendor trademarks as product names; AXI interconnect as Helion IP (Helion's buses are Helion-MM and Helion-ST); vendor bitstreams or encrypted vendor IP attached to issues or releases.

Allowed: backends for vendor families built only on open bitstream documentation: Project X-Ray (Xilinx 7-series), Project IceStorm (iCE40) and Project Trellis (ECP5).

What is original to Helion

  • The HL10 architecture and its parts in HAD, and the FeatureMap bitstream format
  • The HNF netlist, the project and checkpoint formats (.prj, .hckp, .hbits) and the Tcl session
  • The synthesis, implementation, timing, simulation and hardware engines
  • The desktop IDE (helion-ide)

Security

Report vulnerabilities privately through a security advisory; see SECURITY.md for scope. Quality-of-results changes and designs that do not fit a part are not vulnerabilities.
