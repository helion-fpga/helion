Legal fence

Helion is an original FPGA family and CAD, not a wrapper around a vendor tool. Vendor user guides are a checklist of capabilities, not a source tree to copy. Device facts come from HAD, never hardcoded in the CAD. No proprietary vendor IP or databases.

Do not add proprietary vendor databases, vendor Tcl, or UNISIM names. Vendor backends are allowed only on open bitstream databases: the Xilinx 7-series backend is built on Project X-Ray data, never on vendor IP.

Do not ship AXI interconnect as Helion IP. The buses are Helion-MM and Helion-ST.

Do not use vendor trademarks as product names in the UI or docs.

What is original

  • HAD parts (HL10T-C32-1 and kin) and FeatureMap frames
  • HNF netlist, Session Tcl, .hbits, .hckp
  • helion-sim / helion-fabric / IEEE 1149.1 TAP in helion-hw
  • The desktop IDE as an eframe shell over IdeModel
