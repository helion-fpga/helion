# Helion gap ledger

Every gap between Helion and Vivado, or between Helion and "correct and works", goes here
the moment anyone notices it: you, any session, any agent. Started 2026-11-17.

## Rules

1. **Log it right away**, with the numbers and where they were measured. A gap noticed but
   not logged counts as a mistake.
2. **One owner.** Each open gap has one owner: a session, an agent or you.
3. **Closed means fixed AND guarded.** A gap closes only when (a) the fix is committed and
   (b) a **guard** exists that would fail if the gap came back: a test, a benchmark threshold,
   or a checker rule, named in the row. No guard, not closed.
4. **Guards run.** Guards are part of the regular test/benchmark runs, not one-off scripts.
5. **Never delete a row.** Closed rows stay, with the commit and the guard.

Status: `open` (no one on it), `in progress`, `fixed, unguarded`, `closed`.
