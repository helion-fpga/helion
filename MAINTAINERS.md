## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers review and merge pull requests in their areas, triage issues and
help cut releases. To be considered, land engine-backed pull requests, review
other people's, and work within the legal fence. Then ask in
[Discussions](https://github.com/helion-fpga/helion/discussions) or contact the
owner. How decisions are made is in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
